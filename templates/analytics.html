Optimization Formulation & Objective Weights
1. Criticality Risk Index (CRI)

Evaluates \(S = B_{\text{severity}} + \min(25, 1.5 \times \text{overdue}) + \Delta_{\text{TSR}} + \gamma_{\text{GMT}}\). Emergency defects automatically receive top priority scores exceeding 90.

2. Shadow Bundling Factor

Spatial clustering checks \(\Delta km \le 10.0\) and line compatibility. When Civil Engineering occupies a track, Electrical OHE and S&T works are bundled into that exact window to eliminate separate closures.

3. Timetable Constraint Solver

Integrates Control Office Application (COA) timetables to schedule blocks strictly during the gazetted 01:00-04:30 night window and midday lulls, guaranteeing zero detention for high-priority trains like Rajdhani and Vande Bharat.
