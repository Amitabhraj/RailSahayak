Zone Selection Guide
How Zone Filtering Works:

RAIL-SAHAYAK organizes maintenance corridors and block allocations by Railway Administrative Zones.

  • Single Zone: Focus on operations within one specific zone (e.g., North Central Railway).
  • Multiple Zones: Monitor inter-zonal traffic corridors spanning neighboring zones (e.g., NCR and NR).
  • Select All: View whole-network statistics across all operational corridors.
You can switch or change your active zones at any time using the Zone Switcher in the top navigation bar of any page.
